# Preface — a note on the fiction

*AlphaForge, and its paper-trading desk WealthForge, are a teaching team — not a real firm, not a real desk. They exist so that every mechanism in this book has somewhere concrete to live: a ledger, a kill switch, an on-call rotation, a 2:14am page.*

*Their invariants mirror production practice: intent before action; authority before capability; strategies emit intentions, never orders; a signal decided on bar t executes no earlier than bar t+1's open; every bar labeled REAL or SYNTHETIC; paper trading only, always. Their P&L does not exist.*

*No chapter winks at a real system. When the text says "the desk," it means this one.*
